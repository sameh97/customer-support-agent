# Acme Corp — Customer Support FAQ

## Pricing & Plans

**Q: What plans do you offer?**
- Starter ($29/mo): Up to 5 users, core features, email support
- Pro ($79/mo): Up to 25 users, advanced analytics, priority support
- Enterprise: Custom pricing, dedicated account manager, SLA guarantee

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
Yes — all plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

**Q: Can I change my plan anytime?**
Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time from Settings → Billing. Changes apply immediately and billing is prorated.

---

## Billing

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Annual billing gives 20% off.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until end of the billing period. No cancellation fees.

**Q: Where can I find my invoices?**
Settings → Billing → Invoice History. Invoices are also emailed automatically each billing cycle.

## Technical Support

**Q: The app is slow or not loading. What do I do?**
1. Clear browser cache and cookies
2. Try an incognito/private window
3. Switch to Chrome (recommended browser)
4. Check our live status at status.acmecorp.com
5. If the issue persists, email support@acmecorp.com with your account ID

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
Click "Forgot password" on the login screen. A reset link is sent to your registered email within 2 minutes. Check spam if you don't see it.

**Q: Do you have a mobile app?**
Yes — available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Search "Acme Corp".

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**
Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+. Internet Explorer is not supported.

---

## Data & Security

**Q: Where is customer data stored?**
All data is stored on AWS in the eu-west-1 (Ireland) region. We are SOC 2 Type II certified.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
Yes. Settings → Data → Export. You receive a download link within 24 hours. Formats: CSV and JSON.

**Q: Is my data encrypted?**
All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

---

## Onboarding

**Q: How long does onboarding take?**
Most customers are live within 1 business day. Our team contacts you within 2 hours of signup.

**Q: Do you offer training?**
Live onboarding calls are included with Pro and Enterprise. All plans get access to our video library at docs.acmecorp.com.

**Q: Is there an API?**
Yes — full REST API with Swagger docs at api.acmecorp.com/docs. API access is available on Pro and Enterprise plans.
